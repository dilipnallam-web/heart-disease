How Our Stacking Architecture Works

Overcoming the limitations of isolated classifiers through heterogeneous base learners and meta-learning.

⚖️

Class Balancing with SMOTE

Heart disease datasets frequently exhibit class imbalance. We evaluate synthetic oversampling (SMOTE) and random undersampling strictly inside training folds to prevent diagnostic bias.

🔬

Dimensionality Reduction

Comparing full 13-feature baselines with Principal Component Analysis (PCA) and ANOVA F-test SelectKBest feature selection to isolate the strongest cardiovascular risk predictors.

🧬

Stacking Ensemble Meta-Learner

Combining Logistic Regression (linear boundary), Random Forest (non-linear partitions), and SVM (high-margin RBF kernel) via out-of-fold cross-validated meta-estimation.

Dataset Schema

13 Validated Medical Attributes

Comprehensive evaluation incorporating resting hemodynamics, exercise stress indicators, fluoroscopy, and blood biochemistry.

Age & Biological Sex

Demographic baseline for age-adjusted cardiovascular risk stratification.

Chest Pain Type (CP)

Classified into Typical Angina, Atypical Angina, Non-Anginal, and Asymptomatic.

Blood Pressure & Chol

Resting blood pressure in mm Hg and serum cholesterol in mg/dl on admission.

Exercise ECG & ST Slope

Max achieved heart rate, exercise angina, ST depression (oldpeak), and slope.

Fluoroscopy Vessels

Number of major vessels (0-3) colored during cardiac fluoroscopy.

Thalassemia Status

Normal perfusion, fixed myocardial defect, or reversible ischemia defect.
